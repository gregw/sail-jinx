Handicap settings —

The two settings above the retirement box are the handicap variant. Penalties are either a flat number of minutes or a rate per hour of racing; the pool comes back either evenly (γ = 0) or shared by how far behind the leader each boat finished (γ = 1), with anything in between allowed. At γ = 1 the winner gets nothing back and pays its penalty in full. The club default is C — per hour, given back evenly — which is the combination that makes a long night and a short one apply the same correction.
