Import a fleet list

Paste a CSV or choose a file. The first row must be the headings — the column order does not matter and unrecognised columns are ignored. Recognised: sail, name, design, tcf, division, spinnaker, notes (and the usual variants: sail no, boat, class, handicap…).

Every column is optional. Boats already registered are matched, not duplicated — including when a later list fills in a design they were imported without.
