    Add entrants from a sailing-pf export

    Takes a handicaps-YYYY-MM-DD.json file. Each boat's handicap becomes its TCF for this race and its variant sets spinnaker — this is the import that carries the numbers.

    Boats are matched by id first, then through the alias file; anything unknown is added to the register. Boats already entered have their TCF updated and keep their place, and boats missing from the file are left alone — a handicap export does not say who is racing tonight.
